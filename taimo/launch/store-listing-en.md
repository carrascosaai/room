Before pasting:

### Description (max 4000) — 3450 characters

```text
Pay with your phone and Taimo logs it for you. It never connects to your bank and never asks for your bank passwords.

Taimo is a free app to keep track of your spending without effort: it tells you how much you have left this month, splits costs with your partner, flatmates or friends, and lets you know when there's money you can get back. Big text, few buttons, 20 languages.

⚡ PAYMENTS THAT LOG THEMSELVES
• On iPhone: set up a Shortcuts automation with Wallet once, and every Apple Pay payment is logged on its own, without opening the app.
• With Siri: "Hey Siri, log an expense in Taimo", then say "50 euros on food". Perfect for cash.
• Paid with your plastic card? Copy your bank's notice or text message and tap "Paste bank notice": the expense fills itself in. And if you like, Taimo reminds you every night.
• Snap the receipt: Taimo reads the amount, shop, date and items right on your phone.
• Import your bank statement (CSV, OFX, QIF, CAMT.053 or MT940) to bring in everything else.
• Taimo guesses the category, learns from your corrections and avoids duplicates.

🍽️ PAY YOUR PART
Dinner with friends? Snap the receipt and show a QR code. Everyone scans it with their camera, taps what they had and pays you their part by Bizum, PayPal, Revolut or bank transfer. They don't need to install anything. They let you know on WhatsApp and Taimo records who has paid.

💸 MONEY THAT'S YOURS
Taimo checks your payments and tells you about:
• Double charges on the same day.
• Subscriptions that go up in price or start charging you.
• Purchases you can still return, with the deadline.
• Flights where you could claim up to €600 if they were late or cancelled.
• Gadgets still under warranty.
It writes the claim message for you. Everything is worked out on your phone.

👫 COUPLES, FLATS AND TRIPS
• Couples: split even if you're not 50/50 (60/40, 70/30 or by what each of you earns) and see who should pay next.
• Flats: fixed bills like rent or electricity that log themselves every month.
• Trips: group currency, budget per day and per person.
• Shared kitty everyone chips into.
• Split equally, by percentage, by exact amounts or by receipt items.
• Settle up with the fewest transfers. Everyone adds how they get paid (Bizum, IBAN, PayPal or Revolut) and whoever owes pays in one tap from a link, no app needed.
• Invite by WhatsApp with a link or a code and the group syncs on every phone. Only your share counts towards your budget.

📊 YOUR MONEY, CLEAR
• Budget per category and how much you can spend per day.
• Alerts at 80% and 100% of each category.
• Forecast of how you'll end the month.
• Charts by day, week, month and year.
• Subscriptions detected, with a reminder the day before each charge.
• Savings goals, challenges (a week without eating out…) and a wishlist with the 48-hour rule.
• Every expense in hours of work, if you want.
• Ask Taimo: "how much did I spend on food this year?".
• Widget, light and dark mode.

🌍 FOR TRAVEL
155 currencies, converter and the day's rate. Each expense is kept in the currency you paid in and converted to yours.

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY FIRST
• Taimo never asks for your bank passwords and doesn't connect to your bank.
• Your expenses are stored on your phone. They only leave it if you choose: a backup when you sign in with Apple or Google, or a group you share.
• Lock with Face ID or passcode.
• No ads. We don't sell your data.
• Use it without an account.

Free. Made in Spain.
```
