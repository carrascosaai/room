# Competitors of Taimo (automatic expense-tracking / budgeting app for Spain & Europe), as of Oct 2026

> Research caveats. Direct fetches of apps.apple.com, play.google.com, trustpilot.com, elespanol.com and cfnews.net failed (DNS errors in the research environment), so **live App Store / Google Play ratings and review counts could not be read for almost any app**. Store numbers are listed as gaps. Facts come from web-search snippets of the URLs cited. Data older than 2024 is flagged **[OLD]**.

## 1. Fintonic (Spain): business model, traction, funding, controversies

### Takeaway
Fintonic is still Spain's best-known free PFM app with bank aggregation. In practice it has become a **consumer-credit and scoring marketplace**: it earns commissions from banks and lenders, not from users. In 2025 it restructured under a new CEO, left Chile and Mexico, and now presents itself around "scoring crediticio y financiación al consumo". Reviews are polarised. The recurring complaints are loan and offer spam, offer terms that don't match what users are later approved for, and occasional sync breakage with major banks.

### Cited Findings
- **Business model:** the app is free. Revenue comes from the financial-product marketplace through partnership agreements with banks, insurers and lenders, which pay a commission when a user takes out a product — [Fintonic blog "¿Es Fintonic gratis?"](https://www.fintonic.com/blog/es-fintonic-gratis/). The store listing says it charges entities "por igual" when the user chooses a loan — [App Store ES listing](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/fintonic-ahorra-y-fin%C3%A1nciate/id672220319) (via search snippet)
- **2025 restructuring:** Armando Baquero (former head of insurance at Santander) became CEO. The company cut unprofitable businesses and exited countries such as **Chile and Mexico**. It claimed to have **doubled revenue in the last 12 months** and projected profitable growth in 2025. These are company claims, not audited — [El Español, 27 Mar 2025](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/empresas/banca/20250327/fintonic-inicia-nueva-etapa-reestructura-ficha-ceo-armando-baquero-exjefe-seguros-santander/933657123_0.html); [Fintonic News](https://www.fintonic.com/blog/armando-baquero-ceo-fintonic/); [El Asesor Financiero](http://elasesorfinanciero.com/fintonic-inicia-una-nueva-era-bajo-el-liderazgo-de-armando-baquero-como-ceo/)
- **Strategic positioning:** the CEO announcement frames the goal as "consolidar nuestro liderazgo en scoring crediticio y financiación al consumo", which places credit, not PFM, at the core — [Fintonic News](https://www.fintonic.com/blog/armando-baquero-ceo-fintonic/)
- **Traction:** it facilitated **more than €80M in loans in 2024** and connects **4.5M registered users** with 40+ lenders — [El Asesor Financiero](http://elasesorfinanciero.com/fintonic-inicia-una-nueva-era-bajo-el-liderazgo-de-armando-baquero-como-ceo/). The App Store listing says "más de 1 millón de personas confían". Registered and active users clearly differ — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/fintonic-ahorra-y-fin%C3%A1nciate/id672220319)
- **Loans:** Fintonic is an intermediary, not a lender. It shows pre-approved offers of up to €50,000 over up to 84 months, with TAE from 6.99% depending on profile. It can read transactions but cannot move money — [Bankkers](https://bankkers.com/prestamos/fintonic-prestamos/) (commercial blog). Unicaja joined the loan platform — [Estrategias de Inversión](https://www.estrategiasdeinversion.com/actualidad/noticias/empresas/unicaja-banco-se-incorpora-a-la-plataforma-de-n-417979)
- **FinScore / Fintonic Score:** a free credit-quality score. A better score is said to give better loan conditions, although partner banks apply their own criteria, such as ASNEF — [Kelisto](https://www.kelisto.es/finanzas/bancos/fintonic); [asest.es](https://asest.es/story/fintonic-propuesta-de-valor/)
- **Marketing push into consumer credit:** a 2023 Van Gaal campaign — [Merca2, Jun 2023](https://www.merca2.es/2023/06/14/fintonic-barca-consumo-1340565/) **[OLD]**
- **Funding:** a €19M round at a **€160M valuation**, with **ING Ventures holding 22%** and grupo PSN among shareholders. Fintonic also issued up to **€70M of bonds on MARF** (approved by CNMV) — [Capital Riesgo](https://capital-riesgo.es/es/articles/fintonic-capta-19-millones-de-euros-en-una-nueva-ronda-de-financiaci-n-que-valora-la-compa-a-en-160-millones-/) **[OLD, date not shown in snippet; probably 2018–2020]**
- **Trustpilot (ES):** **3.8/5 from about 650 reviews**, 319 of them in the last 12 months, and 97% of negative reviews answered — [Trustpilot ES](https://es.trustpilot.com/review/www.fintonic.com/location/7da06538-e4c2-4c0a-be3e-dd886957cd53). The distribution is very polarised: about 56.7% are 5-star and about 34.8% are 1-star (644 reviews, updated 10 Jul 2026) — [Traders Union](https://tradersunion.com/reviews/www-fintonic-com/)
- **Complaint: offer terms not honoured.** A Jul 2026 2-star review says advertised conditions weren't met. Fintonic replied that initial offers are "estimated best offers" and the final TAE is set by partner banks — [Trustpilot ES](https://es.trustpilot.com/review/www.fintonic.com/location/7da06538-e4c2-4c0a-be3e-dd886957cd53)
- **Complaints: commercial spam for loans, customer service, and imperfect bank integration causing sync errors** — [Chilat blog](https://www.chilat.com/blog/1991691291684896769) (commercial blog, low reliability)
- **Sync outage:** the app stopped showing Santander and BBVA accounts automatically. A beta seemed to fix it, but not at the root — [El Grupo Informático](https://www.elgrupoinformatico.com/apps/fintonic-no-funciona-banco-santander-bbva/) **[OLD / undated]**
- **Missing features:** comparisons say it is the only app offering **free bank sync**, but it has **no tools for planning or projecting future spending**. It also asks for online-banking credentials — [androidguias](https://androidguias.com/alternativas-fintonic/); [blog.mundo-r](https://blog.mundo-r.com/es/apps-controlar-gastos/)
- **Bank coverage and positioning:** Spanish comparisons call it the most-cited PFM in Spain. It works with BBVA, Santander, CaixaBank, ING and others, and duplicate-charge alerts are a strength. The trade-off is in-app loan and insurance offers, and occasional sync failures — [lasfinanzaspersonales.org 2026](https://www.lasfinanzaspersonales.org/post/88-mejores-apps-controlar-gastos-2026); [ahorrainteligent.me](https://ahorrainteligent.me/comparativas/mejores-apps-finanzas-personales/). One 2025 table lists "+30 bancos" and a paid **Pro** version — [comparayahorra.es](https://www.comparayahorra.es/ranking-de-mejores-apps-para-controlar-gastos-y-evitar-deudas-2025/)

### Inferences
- Fintonic's incentives sit with lenders, not with users. Every PFM feature is a funnel into credit, and the 2025 restructuring doubles down on that. A user-paid, no-cross-sell app ("we never sell you a loan") is a clear positioning against it.
- The 4.5M registered vs. "1M+ trust us" gap suggests a large base of dormant or churned users. These are people who have already tried auto-tracking in Spain and left, and so are potentially re-acquirable.
- The product is weak on forward-looking features (forecasting, budgets, planning), which is an obvious opportunity.

### Gaps
- No live App Store ES or Google Play ES rating or review count could be retrieved (fetch blocked).
- No audited 2024/2025 revenue figures. The Registro Mercantil was not checked.
- Price and features of "Fintonic Pro" are unknown.
- Which aggregator or technology it uses (own screen-scraping/PSD2 vs. a third party) is not confirmed.
- No data-protection (AEPD) sanctions or press controversies were found in this search. Absence of results is not proof none exist.

## 2. Pan-European / UK PFM apps: Emma, Snoop, Plum, Cleo, Wallet (BudgetBakers), Spendee

### Takeaway
UK players have moved to **subscription paywalls**: Emma paywalled bank connection at the end of 2022 and became profitable, and Plum reached EBITDA profitability in 2026. Cleo pivoted to the US and is now large ($254M revenue in 2025). Snoop was absorbed by a lender (Vanquis). None of the UK leaders has a meaningful Spanish product. **Wallet by BudgetBakers is the main multi-country app actually used in Spain with bank sync**, behind a paywall. Spendee is small.

### Cited Findings
**Emma (UK)**
- Profitable, with "about half" of users paying since it moved to subscriptions at the end of 2022 and paywalled its core bank-connection feature. This is the founder's own claim — [Fintech Growth Insider podcast](https://www.fintechgrowthinsider.com/p/edoardo-moreni-emma); [LinkedIn E. Moreni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/edoardomoreni/)
- Paying subscribers are "in the hundreds of thousands". A 2024 revenue figure of £5.7M was mentioned by the host but **unverified** — [Fintech Growth Insider](https://www.fintechgrowthinsider.com/p/edoardo-moreni-emma)
- Funding: about $7.3–7.6M in total, including a $2.5M seed (2020, Connect Ventures). No Series B was found — [TechCrunch 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/03/05/emma-seed/) **[OLD]**; [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/emma/__aAzwokGznPh5MMO4Py49mZbL0Mo5n2-w1oeSntO-1Fs)
- Pricing (GBP): Plus £4.99/mo or £41.99/yr; Pro £9.99/mo or £83.99/yr; Ultimate £14.99/mo or £124.99/yr. No EUR pricing was found, and the product is UK-focused (50+ UK banks via UK Open Banking) — [Emma help center](https://help.emma-app.com/en/category/emma-upgrades-1y06pdm/); [coolcuration review](https://coolcuration.com/emma-app-review-uk)

**Snoop (UK)**
- Acquired by **Vanquis Banking Group**, a subprime credit-card lender with 1.7M customers. The price was undisclosed — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/open-banking/vanquis-banking-group-buys-uk-smart-money-app-snoop)
- Earlier: a £10M Seedrs round in 2020 and a £15M Series A at a £47M valuation — [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/money-management-app-snoop-raises-15m/) **[OLD]**

**Plum (UK, also in EU markets)**
- Reached **operational (EBITDA) profitability in January 2026**, with about **£34M ARR**, 60%+ YoY growth, 5.2M downloads and £3.1bn saved and invested — [UKTN, Apr 2026](https://www.uktech.news/fintech/smart-money-app-plum-reaches-profitability-20260428); [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/plum-reports-first-profitability-milestone-as-assets-and-subscriptions-grow/). GetLatka's "$73M ARR" conflicts with this and is unreliable — [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/withplum.com)
- Ran a crowdfunding round in May 2026 — [Crowdcube](https://www.crowdcube.com/companies/plum/pitches/Zpwovl)

**Cleo (UK-born, US-focused)**
- 2025 revenue was **$254M (+87%)**, with more than **1M paid subscribers** and $138M gross profit. These are company-reported figures — [Cleo 2025 annual report](https://web.meetcleo.com/2025-annual-report). Sacra estimated $280M ARR in Jul 2025 — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/cleo/)
- Cleo is US-focused and reportedly "eyes return to the UK" — [Redbus](https://redbus.uk/us-focused-ai-fintech-cleo-hits-150m-arr-and-eyes-return-to-the-uk/)

**Wallet by BudgetBakers (Czechia)**
- More than 6M downloads — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/budgetbakers/195853). Premium starts at about $3.59/mo (third-party figure, Singapore) — [CityNomads](https://citynomads.com/the-best-free-budgeting-apps-in-singapore-to-manage-your-spending/)
- Bank sync is **paid only** (about 4,000 banks claimed). Spanish comparisons recommend it for advanced charts and budgets — [androidguias](https://androidguias.com/alternativas-fintonic/); [lasfinanzaspersonales.org](https://www.lasfinanzaspersonales.org/post/88-mejores-apps-controlar-gastos-2026)

**Spendee (Czechia)**
- Freemium, with only about $654K raised (2017 accelerator) — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/spendee). It is separate from BudgetBakers; no acquisition was found — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/budgetbakers-vs-spendee)

### Inferences
- Users will pay for PFM in Europe (Emma, Plum), but only after a hard paywall on the "magic" feature, which is bank sync. A Spanish entrant can follow the same playbook.
- Snoop being bought by a subprime lender mirrors Fintonic. Free PFMs tend to end up as credit funnels, which feeds user distrust.
- The UK leaders are not localised for Spain (no EUR pricing, UK Open Banking only). This leaves the Spanish market to Fintonic, bank apps and generic multi-country apps.

- Which aggregator Wallet uses in Spain, and how well Spanish banks sync, is not verified.
- Spendee's current status (active or stagnating) and Emma's EU coverage are unknown.
- Money Manager (Realbyte), Monefy and Cashual have no pricing or download data. They are only mentioned as manual, no-bank-link options recommended for privacy — [lasfinanzaspersonales.org](https://www.lasfinanzaspersonales.org/post/88-mejores-apps-controlar-gastos-2026); [androidguias](https://androidguias.com/alternativas-fintonic/). ## 3. French and LatAm aggregators: Bankin', Linxo, Finerio

### Takeaway
Bankin' and Linxo, the French equivalents of Fintonic, both drifted toward B2B open-banking infrastructure and corporate ownership. Linxo has been majority-owned by Crédit Agricole since 2020. Bankin' split off its B2B arm, Bridge, and sold the consumer app to a listed retailer. Finerio (Mexico) pivoted to B2B "Finerio Connect". The lesson: standalone free aggregator PFMs have struggled to monetise B2C.

### Cited Findings
- **Linxo:** majority-owned by Crédit Agricole since 2020. Its revenue is not disclosed, and B2B drives the business — [iphonesoft](https://iphonesoft.fr/2020/01/28/credit-agricole-rachete-agregateur-linxo); [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/rachete-par-le-credit-agricole-comment-linxo-compte-accelerer-dans-lopen-banking/390791) **[OLD]**. It claimed 2.5M users in France in 2019 — [Moneyvox](https://www.moneyvox.fr/banque/actualites/72155/budget-linxo-revendique-2,5-millions-usagers) **[OLD]**. It reportedly launched a redesigned "Linxo Lab" app on 26 Jan 2026 (low-reliability promo site) — [vitepromo](https://www.vitepromo.com/finance-crypto/code-promo-linxo)
- **Bankin':** splitting B2C from B2B (Bridge). The B2C app would be bought by "le géant coté de la distribution" while Bridge raises VC — [CFNews](https://www.cfnews.net/L-actualite/Capital-innovation/Operations/1er-tour/Bankin-va-se-scinder-396136) (date not visible). It claimed 6M+ users in 2021 — [Moneyvox](https://www.moneyvox.fr/actu/75573/bankin-linxo-les-agregateurs-ont-ils-encore-un-avenir) **[OLD]**. It raised €8.4M to become a "coach financier" — [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/bankin-leve-84-millions-deuros-pour-devenir-un-coach-financier/274274) **[OLD]**
- **Finerio (Mexico):** a PFM since 2016 with about 350K users. It launched B2B Finerio Connect in 2020 (50+ institutional clients by 2021) and raised $6.5M in 2023 led by Third Prime — [TechCrunch Oct 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/23/fintech-finerio-6-5m-open-finance-latin-america/); [BusinessWire 2021](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210517005141/en/Finerio-and-Guiabolso-Sign-Partnership-to-Expand-Pioneering-Open-Banking-Solutions-Throughout-Latin-America)

### Inferences
- The aggregator-PFM model in continental Europe tends to end in either a bank acquisition (Linxo) or a B2B pivot (Bridge, Finerio). Taimo needs a B2C monetisation answer from day one, such as subscription.

### Gaps
- The identity of Bankin's B2C buyer (believed to be a large listed French retailer) and the deal date were not confirmed, because the source fetch failed.
- No 2024–2026 user numbers for Bankin' or Linxo.

## 4. Spanish and European bank apps and neobanks: BBVA, CaixaBank, Santander, ING, Openbank, imagin, Revolut, N26

### Takeaway
Bank apps are the default "free" competitor. BBVA has the strongest PFM: automatic categorisation, ML forecasting of upcoming charges, and "Mis otros bancos" aggregation. Neobanks (Revolut Analytics and budgets, N26 Spaces and Insights) categorise only their own accounts. Their structural limit is that each bank sees only its own data well, and third-party data arrives with less detail.

### Cited Findings
- **BBVA "Mi día a día":** an automatic monthly report of income and expenses by category, with recategorisation and per-subcategory budgets — [BBVA Perú infographic](https://www.bbva.com.pe/content/dam/public-web/peru/images/infographics/Mi-dia-a-dia.pdf). In Spain it identifies more than 1,200 specific merchants — [Estrategias de Inversión](https://www.estrategiasdeinversion.com/actualidad/noticias/otras/bbva-espana-anade-una-nueva-herramienta-en-su-n-517727)
- **BBVA forecasting:** shows forecast transactions for the current and next month using ML ensembles that predict recurring charges, amounts and dates, including subscriptions — [BBVA: algoritmos IA](https://www.bbva.com/es/innovacion/que-algoritmos-de-inteligencia-artificial-utiliza-bbva-para-impulsar-las-finanzas-de-sus-clientes/)
- **BBVA "Mis otros bancos":** shows balances and transactions from other banks using the user's credentials. Third-party data is limited to amount, account or card, and a text description, so BBVA had to train a model to infer categories from the description — [BBVA: abre su app a otras entidades](https://www.bbva.com/es/bbva-abre-su-app-a-productos-de-otras-entidades-financieras/); [BBVA: IA detrás de la mejor app](https://www.bbva.com/es/la-inteligencia-artificial-detras-de-la-mejor-app-mundial-de-banca/)
- **Revolut:** analytics filter by category, merchant and country, with recategorisation and custom categories — [Revolut help](https://help.revolut.com/help/app-features/budgeting-and-analytics/managing-analytics-categories/). Per-category budget limits are reportedly on paid plans (Plus/Premium) — [Freenance 2026](https://freenance.io/integrations/revolut-budget-tracking-complete-guide-2026/) (competitor source)
- **N26:** Insights auto-categorises spending. Spaces and Shared Spaces show insights compared with the three-month average, plus six-month history — [N26 blog](https://n26.com/en-de/blog/new-in-n26-more-clarity)
- **Limitation:** a bank app's breakdown may cover only "40–70% of real spending" when users have other banks or cards — [Freenance 2026](https://freenance.io/guides/does-your-bank-app-do-budgeting-enough-2026-europe-7-limitations-revolut-n26-bunq-vs-pfm/) (PFM vendor, commercial bias)

### Inferences
- Spanish users typically have multiple banks (payroll bank, Revolut or N26 for travel, a credit card). A neutral, cross-bank view is something no single bank app is motivated to do well, and BBVA's aggregation degrades for non-BBVA data.
- BBVA sets the bar for free features (categorisation and forecasting). Taimo must at least match this to justify a subscription.

### Gaps
- No specific data was found for CaixaBank (Now/imagin), Santander, ING Spain or Openbank PFM features or aggregation. A dedicated search is needed.
- Bank app store ratings were not retrieved.

## 5. US reference apps: YNAB, Monarch, Copilot, Mint shutdown

### Takeaway
In the US, the subscription PFM is a proven model at about $95–109/yr. Mint's March 2024 closure (ad and referral model, folded into Credit Karma) pushed users to paid apps and validated "pay for privacy and quality". Monarch raised $75M at an $850M valuation in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Pricing:** YNAB is about $109/yr with a 34-day trial and sharing with up to 6 people. Monarch is $99.99/yr, with Monarch Plus at $199/yr. Copilot is about $95/yr or $13/mo and is iOS/Mac-only — [costbench YNAB](https://costbench.com/software/personal-finance/ynab); [getfinny Monarch pricing 2026](https://getfinny.app/blog/monarch-money-pricing-2026); [asianefficiency](https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/copilot-money-vs-monarch-vs-ynab/)
- **Monarch:** raised a $75M Series B in May 2025 (FPV and Forerunner) at an $850M post-money valuation, about $95M raised in total — [startupintros](https://startupintros.com/news/2025-05-01-monarch-money-series-b); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/monarch-money/)
- **Mint:** Intuit shut it down, moving features to Credit Karma (acquired in 2020). Mint earned money through ads and referrals. It had 3.6M MAU in 2021, and development had slowed. Credit Karma does not offer monthly or category budgets. The closure date moved from 1 Jan 2024 to **23 Mar 2024** — [LogRocket](https://blog.logrocket.com/product-management/why-is-the-mint-app-shutting-down/); [Clark](https://clark.com/personal-finance-credit/budgeting-saving/mint-shuts-down/); [Slashdot](https://news.slashdot.org/story/23/11/02/2042229/mint-is-shutting-down-and-its-pushing-users-toward-credit-karma)

### Inferences
- Mint is the cautionary tale for Fintonic's model: a free, referral-funded PFM gets deprioritised in favour of the credit-referral product.

### Gaps
- Copilot funding and user numbers were not found. None of these apps supports Spanish banks; this is believed but not verified.

## 6. Transaction capture, PSD2 friction and the gap no one fills

### Takeaway
All automatic apps depend on PSD2/AISP aggregation or credentials. The EBA extended the reconnection window from 90 to 180 days, but repeated re-consent remains a structural pain. The market gap in Spain is an app that is **automatic, cross-bank, reliable, forward-looking and free of conflicts of interest**: no loan upsell, no data monetisation, and a Spanish UX at a fair EUR price.

### Cited Findings
- The EBA amended the SCA RTS to extend the account-access exemption **from 90 to 180 days**, and made it mandatory when access goes through an AISP. Consultation ran in 2021, the final draft came in 2022, and the change was published in late 2022 — [Vixio](https://www.vixio.com/insights/pc-90-becomes-180-eba-makes-key-sca-change); [Crowdfund Insider, Dec 2022](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/12/199816-european-banking-authority-amends-regulatory-technical-standards-on-authentication-open-banking-provider-tink-comments/); [Finreg360](https://finreg360.com/alerta/la-eba-plantea-modificar-la-autenticacion-reforzada-de-clientes-en-la-psd2/)
- ETPPA said 180 days "is helpful, but does not resolve the AISPs' problem of having to onboard their customers again and again" — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/12/199816-european-banking-authority-amends-regulatory-technical-standards-on-authentication-open-banking-provider-tink-comments/)
- **How each type of app captures transactions:**
  - **Bank aggregation:** Fintonic (free) and Wallet (paid).
  - **Manual or CSV entry:** Monefy, Money Manager, Cashual and Neto, chosen when users don't want to connect a bank — [lasfinanzaspersonales.org](https://www.lasfinanzaspersonales.org/post/88-mejores-apps-controlar-gastos-2026); [androidguias](https://androidguias.com/alternativas-fintonic/)
  - **Own data plus aggregation:** bank apps — [BBVA](https://www.bbva.com/es/bbva-abre-su-app-a-productos-de-otras-entidades-financieras/)

### Inferences (gap analysis)
- **Trust and conflict of interest:** Fintonic, Snoop/Vanquis and Mint/Credit Karma all monetise through credit referrals. No strong Spain-localised player sells PFM as a pure subscription.
- **Reliability:** sync breaks (the Fintonic–Santander/BBVA case) and periodic re-consent are the top functional pain. Proactive reconnection UX, health status per bank, and fallbacks (CSV import, email or notification parsing) are differentiators.
- **Forward-looking features:** Fintonic lacks planning. BBVA forecasts, but only well for BBVA data. A cross-bank forecast (upcoming charges, end-of-month balance, subscription detection) is unoccupied in Spain.
- **Categorisation of non-own-bank data** is hard even for BBVA, which had to build ML for descriptions. Strong merchant normalisation for Spanish descriptors (Bizum, TPV codes, domiciliaciones) is a defensible moat.
- **Privacy-first users** currently fall back to manual apps (Neto, Monefy). An automatic app with credible privacy (EU hosting, no data resale, explicit consent) bridges both segments.
- **Paywalling sync works** (Emma: about half of users paying) but erodes against free Fintonic in Spain. Pricing will need to be clearly below UK/US levels; a hypothesis is €2–5/mo.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/SpainFIRE, r/eupersonalfinance) threads were retrieved, so community sentiment remains unquantified.
- No Xataka or Genbeta 2024–2026 coverage was retrieved.
- Which PSD2 aggregator providers (Tink, GoCardless/Nordigen, Salt Edge, Afterbanks/Unnax, etc.) each competitor uses is not verified.
- Store ratings and review counts for every app are missing because the store fetch was blocked. This needs manual collection from App Store ES and Google Play ES.
